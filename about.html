Chat & Communication
How to extract tasks from WhatsApp and email chats using AI
Most working professionals receive 50-100 messages per day across WhatsApp groups, email threads and team chats. Hidden inside these conversations are tasks, deadlines and commitments that are easy to miss — and costly when forgotten.

The traditional approach is to manually read through every message, highlight important points and transfer them to a to-do list. This typically takes 20-30 minutes per day and is prone to human error — especially when messages come in rapidly during meetings or travel.

AI-powered chat analysis changes this completely. By pasting a conversation into VORA's Chat Analyser, the AI identifies every action item, deadline and commitment in seconds — regardless of how casually or indirectly it was mentioned in the chat.

What kinds of tasks does AI detect?

Modern AI language models understand context and intent, not just keywords. This means VORA can detect tasks even when they are phrased casually or indirectly:

  • Direct assignments: "Priya, please send the report by Friday evening"
  • Meeting requests: "Can we call Monday at 11am to discuss the proposal?"
  • Implicit deadlines: "The client presentation is next Wednesday"
  • Follow-ups: "Let me know once the budget is approved"
  • Commitments: "I'll get back to you by tomorrow morning"

How to use VORA's Chat Analyser

Open WhatsApp Web or your email client, select the conversation, copy all the text, and paste it into VORA's Chat screen. The AI processes the text and shows you a clean list of extracted items with dates, priority levels and the person who assigned each task.

You can then select multiple items and save them to your VORA Calendar with their correct dates and times. For items you want in Google Calendar, use the Schedule screen to build a time-blocked plan and download the .ics file.

Screenshot analysis

If you are on mobile and cannot easily copy text, VORA also accepts screenshots. Take a screenshot of your WhatsApp chat, upload it in the Chat screen, and the AI will read the image and extract tasks the same way. This works on printed messages, forwarded screenshots and even group chat summaries.

The result is a significant reduction in the time spent reading and re-reading messages to figure out what you need to do — letting you focus on actually doing the work.

Document Management
Scan any document with your phone and convert it to Word, PDF or Excel
Paper documents remain common in Indian workplaces — contracts, forms, handwritten notes, meeting minutes and receipts. Converting these to digital formats used to require a flatbed scanner or a dedicated scanning app. AI changes that entirely.

With VORA's document scanner, you simply photograph any document with your phone camera and the AI extracts all the text using optical character recognition (OCR). Within seconds you have the full text ready to download as a PDF with the original image, a Word file for editing, or an Excel spreadsheet for structured data.

This is particularly useful for professionals who receive printed reports, attend meetings with physical handouts, or need to digitise old records without access to a scanner.

What documents work best?

VORA's AI-OCR performs well on a wide variety of document types:

  • Printed documents: Reports, letters, invoices, forms — anything with typed text
  • Handwritten notes: Meeting notes, personal journals, lecture notes (clear handwriting gives best results)
  • Whiteboards: Brainstorming sessions, diagrams with text, flow charts
  • Receipts and bills: Extract line items for expense tracking
  • Business cards: Extract name, phone number and email

Tips for best results

For the most accurate text extraction, photograph the document in good lighting with the camera held flat above the page. Avoid shadows, glare from overhead lights, and ensure the full document is within the frame. A distance of 30-40cm from the page works well for A4 documents on most phone cameras.

Saving original scans

In addition to extracted text, VORA allows you to save the original scan as a PDF — meaning the actual photograph of the document is embedded in a PDF file without any changes. This is useful when you need to share the original document digitally while retaining its appearance exactly as printed or handwritten.

Privacy and security

Images uploaded to VORA are processed in your browser session and are not permanently stored on any server. Basic plan users should download their files before closing the app, as session-only storage means files are cleared when the session ends. Premium plan users have permanent storage for their scan history.

Time Management
How AI builds a realistic time-blocked schedule for your workday
Time blocking — allocating specific time slots to specific tasks — is one of the most effective productivity techniques recommended by researchers and business leaders. The challenge is that building a good time-blocked schedule manually takes time and mental effort that most people do not have first thing in the morning.

VORA's Smart Scheduler solves this by letting you describe your tasks in plain language and having AI build a realistic, time-blocked schedule in seconds. The AI accounts for task complexity, includes breaks, and arranges similar tasks together to minimise context switching.

The output is a detailed day plan with specific time slots for each task, which you can download as a .ics calendar file and open directly in Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Microsoft Outlook.

What makes a good AI-generated schedule?

The quality of your schedule depends on the detail you provide. Instead of simply listing tasks, include approximate durations, any fixed time commitments (calls, meetings) and your preferred start time. The more context you give, the more realistic the output:

  • Less specific: "Write report, attend team meeting, reply emails"
  • More specific: "Write Q1 sales report (2-3 hours), team meeting at 3pm (1 hour), reply urgent emails (30 min), review presentation draft (45 min), start at 9am"

Syncing with Google Calendar

After VORA generates your schedule, tap the Download .ics button. This creates a standard calendar file that contains all your time blocks as individual events. On Android, tap the downloaded file and it will ask which calendar app to open it with — select Google Calendar. On iPhone, the file opens in Apple Calendar automatically.

Each task appears as a separate event in your calendar with its start time, end time and description — giving you a visual overview of your day alongside any existing appointments.

Using chat tasks in the scheduler

One of the most powerful workflows in VORA is combining the Chat Analyser with the Scheduler. First, extract tasks from your WhatsApp or email using the Chat screen. Then tap "Open Schedule" to send the selected tasks directly to the Schedule screen pre-filled and ready to build. This takes your scattered chat commitments and turns them into a structured, timed day plan in under a minute.

5 ways busy professionals in India are using AI assistants to save time every day
Artificial intelligence tools have moved from being interesting novelties to genuine time-savers for working professionals across industries. Here are five practical ways people are using AI personal assistants to reduce workload and stay on top of responsibilities.

Whether you are in sales, operations, education, healthcare or any other field, the common thread is the same — too much information coming in, too little time to process it, and the constant risk of missing something important. AI assistants address each of these pain points directly.

1. Extracting action items from group chats

Sales teams, project managers and business owners often have multiple WhatsApp groups running simultaneously. Important instructions, deadlines and commitments get buried under dozens of messages within minutes. Using VORA's Chat Analyser to scan group conversations at the start and end of the workday ensures nothing is missed — even in high-volume groups.

2. Preparing for client meetings in 30 seconds

Before any client call or meeting, professionals use VORA's Topic Brief feature to get a quick summary of the company, industry or topic they are about to discuss. Entering the client's company name gives a current, factual brief covering recent news, business focus and relevant background — all in under 30 seconds. This replaces 15-20 minutes of manual research.

3. Digitising paper documents on the go

Field sales executives, teachers and healthcare workers regularly encounter paper forms, reports and documents that need to be shared digitally. Instead of carrying a scanner or returning to the office, they photograph documents with their phone and use VORA to convert them to PDF or Word format instantly — then share via email or WhatsApp.

4. Building a daily plan from scattered tasks

Rather than manually planning their day in a diary or planner, busy professionals describe their tasks to VORA in the morning and receive a complete time-blocked schedule. The AI decides the optimal order, allocates realistic time slots and adds breaks — saving 20-30 minutes of planning time and reducing decision fatigue at the start of the day.

5. Writing personalised reminders for important events

Generic calendar reminders say "Meeting at 3pm." VORA's Smart Reminder writes a contextual note specific to your event — who it is with, what to prepare, what to bring, and why it matters. This means when the reminder fires, it actually helps you prepare rather than just alerting you that something is happening.

These five uses represent the core of what AI personal assistants offer working professionals today — not replacement of human judgment, but a significant reduction in the time and mental energy spent on routine information processing tasks.
