Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026  ·  Apexlogic / VORA  ·  apex-vora.vercel.app

VORA is an AI-powered personal assistant operated by Apexlogic (India), led by CEO Madhuri Shukla. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use VORA.

1. Information We Collect

Trial users: Name, email address, and city — collected when starting a 1-day free trial. No verification required.

Registered users: Full name, email address, mobile number, city, and password (stored as a hashed value). Collected during sign-up for 30-day free access.

Usage data: Session information (login time, plan type, expiry date) stored locally on your device using browser localStorage. We do not maintain a centralised database of user activity.

2. How AI Features Use Your Data

VORA's AI features send your input to the Anthropic Claude API for processing:

  • Chat Analyser: Text you paste or screenshots you upload are sent to Claude for task extraction
  • Scan to PDF: Document images are sent to Claude for OCR text extraction — images are processed in real time and not stored on any server
  • Smart Scheduler: Your task descriptions are sent to Claude to generate time-blocked schedules
  • Topic Brief: Your search query is sent to Claude with web search tools enabled
  • Smart Reminders: Event details are sent to Claude to generate reminder text

None of this data is stored on our servers. Each request is processed and the response is returned directly to your device.

3. Scanned Documents and PDF Files

Document images uploaded for scanning are processed via the Anthropic API and immediately discarded. Generated PDF files exist only in your browser session — they are not uploaded or stored on any server. Basic plan users must download PDFs before closing the app. Premium subscribers will have cloud storage in a future update.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your registration details and session data are stored in your browser's localStorage. This data remains on your device only. We do not have access to your local storage. All API calls to Anthropic are made over HTTPS with TLS encryption.

5. Advertising

VORA may display advertisements via Google AdSense. Google may use cookies to personalise ads based on your browsing activity. You can opt out of personalised ads at google.com/settings/ads.

6. Subscriptions and Payments

VORA subscriptions (₹25/month Basic, ₹99/month Premium) are processed manually via email correspondence with Apexlogic. We do not collect or store payment card information. UPI payments are made directly to Apexlogic's UPI ID — we do not handle payment data.

7. Children's Privacy

VORA is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided their information, please contact us immediately for deletion.

8. Your Rights

You may delete your account data at any time by clearing your browser's localStorage for this site, or by contacting us. We will confirm deletion within 48 hours.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Continued use of VORA after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. Significant changes will be communicated via the app's About section.
