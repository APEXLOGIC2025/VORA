Privacy Policy
Last updated: March 2026 · Apexlogic / VORA

VORA is an AI-powered personal assistant operated by Apexlogic (India). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use VORA at apex-vora.vercel.app.

1. Information We Collect

When you register, we collect your name, email address, mobile number, and city. For trial users we collect name, email and city only. We do not collect payment information directly — payments are handled via email correspondence.

2. How We Use AI Features

VORA's AI features (Topic Brief, Chat Analyser, Scheduler, Reminders, Scan to PDF) send your input text or images to the Anthropic Claude API for processing. This data is used only to generate your response and is not stored on our servers. Scanned document images are processed in real time and immediately discarded.

3. Data Storage

Your registration details and session information are stored locally on your device using browser storage. We do not maintain a centralised database of user activity. Scanned PDFs are session-only and are not stored on our servers — you must download them before closing the app.

4. Advertising

VORA may display advertisements via Google AdSense. Google may use cookies to personalise ads. You can opt out at google.com/settings/ads.

5. Children's Privacy

VORA is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

6. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion: apexlogic2025@gmail.com or ceo@apexlogic.in
